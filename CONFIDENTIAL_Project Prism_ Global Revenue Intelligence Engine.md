# **Project Prism: Global Revenue Intelligence Engine**

**Project Team:** 

Pete Mansel (CRO), Mike Duda (VP RevOps), Brad Towers (SE Leader UK/ANZ), Steve Logan (CIO)

**Status:** Concept / Prototype Phase

### **The Vision**

To take the single beam of raw data currently sitting in Salesforce and **refract it into a clear spectrum of strategic insights** by region. Project Prism leverages **Generative AI (Claude)** to turn unstructured field notes into a competitive weapon for the ELT and GTM Leadership.

### **The Problem: Data Rich, Insight Poor**

We will now capture thousands of data points regarding deal health and outcomes:

1. **Win/Loss Notes:** The raw "ground truth" from the field.  
2. **Deal Review Forms:** The new structured methodology inside SFDC.

**The Gap:** This data is largely unstructured text. It is impossible for humans to manually read, synthesize, and categorize every single note from every global opportunity on a weekly basis. Valuable intelligence is currently "locked" in text fields, rendering us reactive rather than proactive.

### **The Solution: AI-Driven "Harvesting"**

By combining **Sales Engineering technical expertise** (Brad’s AI coding capabilities with Claude) with **RevOps strategy** (Mike’s process ownership), and **IT Instrumentation** (Steve) we will build a custom internal app that:

* **Ingests:** Automatically pulls raw text data from SFDC Win/Loss notes and Opportunity Deal Reviews / Clari Co-Pilot call transcripts.  
* **Analyzes:** Uses Large Language Models (Claude) to read context, sentiment, and nuance across every single opportunity on the planet.  
* **Synthesizes:** Aggregates this data into a structured Executive Report.

### **The Deliverable: The Weekly Global Intel Report**

Every week, the ELTand GTM Leadership will receive a condensed dossier containing:

* **Global Win/Loss Themes:** A region-by-region breakdown (NA, UK, ANZ) of *why* we are winning or losing. This goes beyond checkboxes, identifying trends in pricing, product gaps, or competitor tactics.  
* **"At Risk" Radar:** The AI will scan *active* deals for risk markers hidden in the notes (e.g., negative sentiment, stalled stakeholders, lack of executive alignment) that may not be reflected in the standard forecast stage.  
* **Market Signals:** Early detection of new competitor FUD (Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt) or emerging customer objections.

### **Strategic Impact & ROI**

1. **Forecast Accuracy:** Move from "gut feel" to data-backed risk assessment on the pipeline.  
2. **Product Feedback Loop:** faster identification of product gaps killing deals.  
3. **Sales Coaching:** Identify regions or reps struggling with specific parts of the sales motion based on deal review narratives to feed back into our Enablement program.

### **Timeline & Next Steps**

* **Phase 1 (Week 1):** Team to define technical schema and data export requirements.  
* **Phase 2 (Week 2):** Build "Project Prism" Prototype on Claude; test against historical NA/UK/ANZ data.  
* **Phase 3 (Week 3):** Present first "Global Intel" draft report to ELT for feedback.

# **Project Prism: The 3-Horizon Strategy**

**Executive Summary** Project Prism is not just a reporting tool; it is the foundation for an autonomous RevOps infrastructure. We are moving from **Passive Insight** (Horizon 1\) to **Active Intervention** (Horizon 2\) to **Autonomous Scale** (Horizon 3).

### **Horizon 1: The Intelligence Engine (Now \- Q1 ‘26)**

*Goal: Visibility & Synthesis* We establish the "Brain." We prove that AI can reliably interpret our complex sales data and provide ELT-level strategic value.

* **Core Input:** SFDC Win/Loss Notes, Deal Review Forms, Capri Co-Pilot call transcripts..  
* **The Engine:** Custom Claude app (Project Prism) managed by Sales Engineering & RevOps.  
* **The Output:** Weekly Global Intel Report.  
  * *Win/Loss Analysis:* Why we win/lose by region.  
  * *Risk Radar:* Identification of at-risk deals based on note sentiment.  
* **Success Metric:** ELT & GTM Leaders leverage weekly report for forecast calls; 90% accuracy in risk identification.

### **Horizon 2: The Intervention Layer (Q2 ‘26)**

*Goal: Automation & Action* We connect the "Brain" to the "Hands." Instead of just reporting problems, the system begins to fix them automatically within the workflow.

* **Expanded Inputs:** Ingesting **Clari Copilot** call transcripts (customer voice) to validate rep notes against actual conversation reality.  
* **The Mechanism:** Integration with **Salesforce Agentforce**.  
* **The Output:** Automated corrective actions.  
  * *Auto-Tasks:* If Prism detects a stalled deal in the notes, Agentforce auto-creates a task: *"Schedule Executive alignment call."*  
  * *Coaching Nudges:* If Prism detects a rep struggling with pricing objections in transcripts, it auto-sends an enablement one-pager to the rep.  
* **Success Metric:** Reduction in "stalled deal" time; increase in deal velocity.

### **Horizon 3: The Agentic Growth Force (H2 ‘26)**

*Goal: Infinite Scale & Market Penetration* We deploy an "Army of Agents" to decouple revenue growth from headcount. We shift the heavy lifting of finding and landing new business from expensive human labor to autonomous digital workers, radically optimizing **RAC (Revenue Acquisition Cost).**

* **The Vision:** A fleet of 1,000+ Agentic Sales Agents driving New Logo Acquisition (NCA) and Expansion.  
* **Capabilities:**  
  * **"Hunter" Agents (NCA):** Autonomous agents that own the top of the funnel. They scan regions, identify intent signals, personalize outreach, and execute qualification email conversations 24/7. They hand off only warm, qualified meetings to human AEs.  
  * **"Account Manager" Agents (Upsell/Cross-sell):** Agents that constantly monitor our current customer base for "White Space" opportunities. They automatically identify accounts ripe for new products and initiate expansion plays without waiting for a human Account Manager to review the account.  
* **Strategic Impact:**  
  * **Total Addressable Market (TAM) Coverage:** We can touch 100% of our TAM, not just the accounts a human rep has time to call.  **Go after the 4.7 Million Fred referenced at GTMKO**.  
  * **Efficiency:** Drastic reduction in labor costs per new pipeline dollar.  
* **Success Metric:** 50% reduction in RAC; 10x increase in outbound territory coverage.
